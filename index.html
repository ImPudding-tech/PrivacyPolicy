1. Information We Collect

When you register and interact with our automated Facebook Messenger Service, we collect limited personal data required to provide discount codes, keep track of points, and confirm account identities:

  • Page-Scoped ID (PSID): A unique numeric identifier provided by Meta Platforms to manage your chat session.
  • Contact Email Address: Provided during account onboarding to confirm discount coupon ownership.
  • Title and Last Name: Honorifics (e.g., Elder Smith or Sister Johnson) used for personalized chat responses.
  • Batch Information: Missionary arrival/departure schedule (month and year) for batch verification.
  • Referral Data: System-generated invitation codes and associated point balances.

2. How Information Is Used

Your data is strictly utilized to operate and enhance our referral engine and discount rewards store:

  • Issuing instant 10% welcome vouchers upon registration completion.
  • Calculating Level-1 (Direct) and Level-2 (Tiered) referral points up to the account cap.
  • Redeeming single-item and wholesale discount store coupons inside chat.
  • Re-authenticating returning users via the /start or /points commands.

3. Data Storage & Protection

We implement strict measures to ensure your data remains secure and private:

  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: All user records are stored inside encrypted Google Workspace enterprise databases.
  • No Password Storage: We never collect, request, or store passwords or payment card info.
  • No Third-Party Sales: We do not sell, trade, lease, or monetize user data to third-party ad networks or brokers.

4. Third-Party Integration (Meta / Facebook)

Our service operates using the official Facebook Graph API (v20.0). Messages and interactive button events pass through Meta's secure webhook infrastructure in compliance with Meta's Platform Terms and Data Policy.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion Instructions

You retain full ownership and control over your personal information. If you wish to permanently purge your account data, follow these steps:

How to Request Immediate Data Removal:

  1. Open Facebook Messenger and navigate to our official Facebook Page chat.
  2. Send the message: /delete my data or DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Alternatively, send an email request directly to our team with your registered email address.

Upon confirmation, your PSID, registered email address, title, batch details, and accumulated referral point logs will be permanently deleted from our active database within 48 hours.

6. Contact Information

If you have any questions, feedback, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or data security practices, please contact us:

  • Facebook Page: Send a direct message to our Page on Messenger.
  • Support Availability: Monday – Saturday (Standard Service Hours).
