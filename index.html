This Privacy Policy outlines how the Missionary Perks & Rewards Platform collects, uses, protects, and handles personal information processed through our automated Facebook Messenger Service. We are committed to maintaining data privacy and ensuring complete transparency.

1
Information We Collect

When you register or interact with our automated Messenger chatbot, we collect minimal data strictly required to maintain account balances and authenticate identities:

  • Page-Scoped User ID (PSID): A unique system string assigned by Meta Platforms to route chat responses securely to your Messenger app.
  • Email Address: Validated during registration to confirm discount voucher ownership and prevent abuse.
  • Title & Last Name: Honorific credentials (e.g., Elder Smith or Sister Johnson) to personalize customer care interactions.
  • Missionary Batch Details: Arrival and departure schedules (e.g., August 2026) used for eligibility verification.
  • Referral & Ledger Data: Generated invitation links, point logs, and claimed promotional discount codes.
2
How We Use Your Data

Your data is exclusively processed to power our service capabilities, including:

  • Delivering your instant 10% Welcome Discount Coupon upon successful email registration.
  • Accurately tracking Level-1 (Direct) and Level-2 (Secondary) referral point earnings up to the account cap.
  • Redeeming single-item piece discounts or wholesale order vouchers inside our Messenger store.
  • Restoring your existing account balance when you trigger commands like /points or /store.
3
Data Security & Enterprise Protection

We apply rigid security practices to guarantee your stored data remains uncompromised:

  • Cloud Infrastructure: Information is securely housed within encrypted Google Workspace cloud infrastructure.
  • Zero Sensitive Data Processing: We never collect, store, or request credit cards, passwords, or national identification numbers.
  • No Data Monetization: We strictly prohibit selling, renting, or leasing user data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
4
Meta API Integration

Our application interacts directly with official Meta Graph API endpoints. Message payload routing and webhook events occur in compliance with Meta Commercial Terms and Data Policies.

5
User Rights & Data Deletion Procedure

You have the absolute right to request the total purging of your registered profile and points history from our databases at any time.

⚠️ How to Request Immediate Data Removal:

  1. Open Facebook Messenger and access our official Facebook Page chat.
  2. Type and send either: /delete my data or DELETE ACCOUNT.
  3. Alternatively, reach out directly to our Page Admins via direct message with your registered email address.

Execution Notice: Once requested, your PSID, email, name, batch records, and earned point logs will be permanently deleted from active memory within 48 hours.
